Hotspot Helper

Please note: Coffeebreak Mail does not provide internet. These guides help you share your own phone's mobile data or find free public WiFi. Hotspot data comes out of your phone plan, so check your plan's data allowance and any extra charges with your provider.

iPhone personal hotspot

  1. Open Settings.
  2. Tap Personal Hotspot. On some iPhones it is under Settings, then Mobile Data, then Personal Hotspot.
  3. Turn on Allow Others to Join.
  4. Note the Wi-Fi Password shown on that screen. You can tap it to change it.
  5. On your other device, open the WiFi list, pick your iPhone's name and type the password.
  6. When you finish, turn Allow Others to Join off to save data and battery.

Apple's official Personal Hotspot guide

Android hotspot

  1. Open Settings.
  2. Tap Network and internet, or Connections on Samsung phones.
  3. Tap Hotspot and tethering, or Mobile Hotspot and Tethering on Samsung.
  4. Tap WiFi hotspot or Mobile Hotspot and switch it on.
  5. Tap the hotspot name to see or change the network name and password.
  6. On your other device, join that network with the password. Switch the hotspot off when you finish.

Menu names change a little between phone brands.

Google's official Android hotspot guide

Tips to save data

  • Turn off automatic app updates and phone backups while on a hotspot.
  • Pause cloud photo syncing until you are back on home WiFi.
  • Set video streaming to standard or low quality.
  • Download songs, movies and big files at home or at the library before you go.
  • Close apps you are not using so they stop refreshing in the background.
  • On a laptop, mark the hotspot as a metered connection so it uses less data.
  • Check your data usage in your phone settings and reset the counter each billing month.
  • Use a strong hotspot password and turn the hotspot off when you finish.

Find free public WiFi

  • Newcastle Libraries: free WiFi and computers at library branches.
  • State Library of NSW public library services: find NSW public libraries near you.
  • National and State Libraries Australia: links to every state library.
  • WiFi Map: a map of public WiFi spots shared by users.
  • Many council centres, shopping centres, cafes and transport hubs have free WiFi. Ask staff for the network name.
Stay safe on public WiFi: avoid banking or payments on open networks, check the network name with staff, and forget the network when you leave.
